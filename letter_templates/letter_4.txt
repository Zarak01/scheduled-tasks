Dear [NAME],

Another year has passed from your life.

While people often see such a day only as something to celebrate, perhaps it is also a day to reflect. A year added to our age is, in reality, a year taken from whatever time remains for us in this dunya.

None of us knows how much of that time is left.

May Allah make the years that have passed a proof for you and not against you, forgive your shortcomings, and make whatever remains of your life better than what has passed. May He grant you a heart that remembers Him, a life spent in obedience to Him, and a beautiful ending upon iman.

May we never become so occupied with counting our years that we forget that our years are also being counted down.
