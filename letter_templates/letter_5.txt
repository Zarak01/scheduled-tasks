Dear [NAME],

Alhamdulillah for every year Allah has allowed you to live.

For every morning you were permitted to wake up, every difficulty He carried you through, every opportunity He gave you to repent and improve, and every blessing—known and unknown—that accompanied you along the way.

Growing older is not merely about adding another number to one's age. It is another reminder of just how much Allah has already given us and how deserving He is of our gratitude.

May Allah place barakah in whatever remains of your life, make you increasingly grateful to Him, forgive what has passed, and allow every additional day He grants you to bring you closer to Him.

Alhamdulillah for the life that has passed, and may what remains be spent in a way that pleases the One who gave it.
