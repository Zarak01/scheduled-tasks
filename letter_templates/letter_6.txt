Dear [NAME],

Another year of life has gone by—something that carries both a blessing and a reminder.

A blessing, because Allah allowed you to remain in this world long enough to see days that many others did not reach. A reminder, because with every year we gain in age, we inevitably move closer to the day when our time here will end.

So perhaps the question is not simply, “How old am I now?” but rather, “What have I done with the years Allah has entrusted to me, and what will I do with whatever remains?”

May Allah make your remaining years full of barakah, sincere worship, beneficial deeds, repentance, and goodness. May He allow you to leave behind deeds that continue benefiting you even after you are gone, and when your appointed time eventually comes, may you return to Him while He is pleased with you.

Life is a gift—but it is also an amanah, and every passing year reminds us that the gift was never promised to last forever.
